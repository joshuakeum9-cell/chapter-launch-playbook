When something breaks

Harvest returned almost nothing
A source broke; sites change. Ask Claude which sources returned zero and check those by hand. Do not assume the city went quiet.
A calendar stopped working
Mark it manual in your source registry and check it yourself each week.
Claude keeps asking to use my browser
Normal on Luma, Eventbrite and the submission form. Say yes. It reads pages; it does not click.
A page is asking me to prove I am human
Claude stops and tells you. Open the page yourself and paste what you see. Claude will not solve these.
The finished issue sounds like a robot
The yellow rewrite lines were deleted without rewriting the blocks above them. Those three blocks are the only part readers remember.
A reader emailed me an event
Send them the submission form link at the bottom of any issue. It shows up in the next harvest, marked submitted.
Assembly stopped and said the fixed links are not ready
The plugin refuses to build an issue with a placeholder in the platforms block or the Join the fun block. Remove those two blocks from the JSON and the standard text is inserted with the real links. You never type them.
Formatting broke when I pasted into Substack
Almost always the Word document, or a copy button. Instead:
  1. Open the HTML file in your browser
  2. Click just before the first word at the top
  3. Hold the mouse down and drag to the bottom, past the last line
  4. Copy. Ctrl+C on Windows, Cmd+C on a Mac
  5. Open a new Substack post and paste
Dragging is what carries the formatting and links. A copy button does not.
Claude does not know my chapter settings
Check the settings file is on the project, not a chat. Start a fresh conversation.
I do not have a paid Claude account
The skills need one. Every step can be done by hand: the source list, the weekly sweep, the cut, the write-up. Chapters did it that way before the toolkit. Longer, not worse.
I missed a week
Send the next one. No apology, no explanation. The target is weeks covered over a quarter.
My chapter has a co-lead
Everything works the same. Decide who owns the send.
