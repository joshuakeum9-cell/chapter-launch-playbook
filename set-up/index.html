Step 03 of 05, once

Set up

The first thing you run. You never answer the same question twice.

Chapter setup once

    


    
Eight questions, one message

    
All eight arrive together, each with its default. Answer what you can in one reply. Anything you skip gets the default, and Claude tells you which. Every chapter starts weekly, so how often is not asked.

    

      
    
        
  1. Which city?Becomes your chapter name, Climate Tech [City].
    2. 
        
  2. Your name?Goes on every issue as the sender. Email from a person gets opened.
    3. 
        
  3. Which day to send?Pick one and never move it. No view? Tuesday or Wednesday.
    4. 
        
  4. Which events count as local?The sentence every harvest uses to decide what belongs. Default: the city plus anywhere a subscriber would travel on a weeknight.
    5. 
        
  5. Which web address?Chapters use two patterns and there is no published rule. The one question with no default: recorded, and flagged to confirm with the CTC team.
    6. 
        
  6. How to close each issue?Default: [your first name] and the Climate Tech [City] team.
    7. 
        
  7. Timezone?It offers the obvious one. You confirm.
    8. 
        
  8. Anything unusual about your city?Optional. A second cluster, a university that owns the calendar, a season when everything stops.
    9.
