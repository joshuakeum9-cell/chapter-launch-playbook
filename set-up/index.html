Step 05 of 08 · Once

Set up

The first thing you run. You never answer the same question twice.

Chapter setup · once

    


    
Nine questions

    
One at a time, each with a default. Reply "default" to accept. The last is optional.

    

      
    
        
  1. Which city?Becomes your chapter name, Climate Tech [City].
    2. 
        
  2. Your name?Goes on every issue as the sender. Email from a person gets opened.
    3. 
        
  3. Which day to send?Pick one and never move it.
    4. 
        
  4. How often?Weekly is the standard and the default. Every two weeks is offered only after your first harvests show the volume.
    5. 
        
  5. Which events count as local?The sentence every harvest uses to decide what belongs. It is the only instruction it gets.
    6. 
        
  6. Which web address?Chapters use two patterns and there is no published rule. Recorded, and flagged to confirm with the CTC team.
    7. 
        
  7. How to close each issue?Default: [your first name] and the Climate Tech [City] team.
    8. 
        
  8. Timezone?It offers the obvious one. You confirm.
    9. 
        
  9. Anything unusual about your city?A second cluster, a university that owns the calendar, a season when everything stops. Skippable.
    10.
