Step 01 of 06 · The map

Your first two weeks

Then the same four steps every week.

Week 1Logo, in ChatGPT. Substack, set up by the CTC founders
Week 2Source list, subscriber list, first issue
Every weekHarvest, cut, draft, send*
Every monthRefresh your opportunities
Every three monthsRefresh your source list
OptionalPartners, whenever you want venues and speakers. Not needed to send

* Weekly is the standard and the default. If your first two or three harvests come back with fewer than about five events a week after the cut, the toolkit offers every two weeks. Decided on evidence, never on one busy week.

Everything except the logo runs inside your own Claude account.

Done looks like

  • Logo approved
  • Newsletter sending on a fixed day
  • 25 or more event sources registered
