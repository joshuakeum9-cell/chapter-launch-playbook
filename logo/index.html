Step 01 of 05, week one

Your chapter logo

The one step that does not run in Claude. ChatGPT makes the images.

Every chapter logo is the same lockup: a square icon on the left, Climate Tech [your city] stacked on three lines to the right. Only the building silhouette changes.

The icon on its own

Two planes, a light side and a dark side, a handful of steps. If your landmark still has windows or a many-piece spire, it is not abstract enough.

    The house prompt, your city filled in. Paste it into ChatGPT and ask for images.
