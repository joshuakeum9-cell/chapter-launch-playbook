Partners

Venues, audiences and speakers come from partners. None of it is needed to send the newsletter.

Skip this for as long as you like. The weekly cycle does not read the partner map, and your issues send without it. Come back when you want to host an event, find a speaker, or grow past the list you have. If you do build one, the monthly opportunities run reads it for local programs instead of researching the city again.
