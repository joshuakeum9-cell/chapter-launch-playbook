This Week in Detail

⚡ Grid Interconnection 101

When: Wednesday, Aug 12

Where: Ecotrust Building, 721 NW 9th Ave, Portland

A plain-language walk through how a solar or storage project gets connected to the grid, hosted by the Oregon Clean Power Cooperative. Evening session in the Pearl District, free, with a Q&A at the end for anyone with a project stuck in the queue.

🍻 Mass Timber Happy Hour

When: Wednesday, Aug 12

Where: Loyal Legion, 710 SE 6th Ave, Portland

Portland's timber and building-decarbonization crowd meets over a beer once a month, hosted by the Tall Wood Design Institute. Loyal Legion on SE 6th from 6 pm, free to attend, and drinks are your own. No talks and no agenda, just people who work on buildings and the people who want to.

🤖 AI for Load Forecasting: PSU Energy Seminar

When: Wednesday, Aug 12

Where: Engineering Building, 1930 SW 4th Ave, Portland

A research seminar from Portland State's power engineering group on machine learning for short-term demand forecasting, hosted by the university's energy program. Lunchtime on the downtown campus, free and open to the public, with a hybrid link for anyone who cannot make it in person. More technical than most of what is in this issue.

♻️ Repair Cafe: Small Appliances

When: Thursday, Aug 13

Where: St. Johns Community Center, 8427 N Central St, Portland

Bring a broken toaster, lamp, or kettle and fix it alongside a volunteer, run by Repair PDX. Morning session at the St. Johns Community Center, drop-in, and the listing does not state a price. One of the easiest ways to meet people who care about waste without going to a panel about it.

📊 Cascadia Climate Data Meetup

When: Thursday, Aug 13

Where: WeWork Custom House, 220 NW 8th Ave, Portland

Monthly meetup for people working with climate and energy data, hosted by Cascadia Data Collective. Evening at WeWork Custom House in the Pearl District, free, register for the door list. This month two short talks on wildfire smoke datasets, then open discussion and time to compare notes on whatever you are building.

🌿 Willamette Riverkeeper Work Party

When: Saturday, Aug 15

Where: Willamette Park boat ramp, SW Macadam Ave, Portland

Three hours pulling invasive ivy and blackberry off the riverbank with Willamette Riverkeeper. Meet at the Willamette Park boat ramp in South Waterfront at 9 am, free, all ages. Gloves and tools are provided, so bring water and wear shoes you do not mind ruining.

🎨 Climate Futures Print Studio

When: Saturday, Aug 15

Where: Outlet PDX, 4 SE 28th Ave, Portland

A drop-in screen-printing afternoon where you make a poster about the city you want to live in, run by Outlet's studio team. At Outlet PDX in Kerns, sliding-scale ticket, materials included. Kids are welcome with an adult, and finished posters go up in the studio window for the rest of the month.

🚲 Eastside Greenway Ride

When: Sunday, Aug 16

Where: Meet at Laurelhurst Park, SE 39th Ave and Stark St, Portland

A slow ten-mile group ride along the neighborhood greenways, hosted by BikeLoud PDX, with stops at two recently finished bike-infrastructure projects. Meets at Laurelhurst Park at 10 am, free, bring your own bike and a helmet. The ride ends at a food cart pod, so plan on lunch.

🌾 Tualatin Valley Farm Walk

When: Sunday, Aug 16

Where: Gales Meadow Farm, Forest Grove

A walking tour of a small organic farm west of the city run by Friends of Family Farmers, covering soil health, water use, and what a hot summer did to the season. Ticketed, carpool from Portland arranged on the registration page.
